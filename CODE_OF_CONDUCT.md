# Community Standards

GpTMax welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept review feedback and explain disagreements professionally.
- Protect private information and respect licenses.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.

Unacceptable behavior may result in removal of comments, rejection of contributions, or exclusion from project spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub.

This standard applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
